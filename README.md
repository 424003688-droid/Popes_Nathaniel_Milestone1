• Why is data preprocessing important before performing data mining?

-Data preprocessing is a crucial foundational step in data mining because raw real-world data is often incomplete, noisy, and inconsistent. Unhandled issues—such as missing values, duplicate records, and extreme numerical outliers—can distort statistical analyses and lead to biased or inaccurate model outcomes. By systematically cleaning, standardizing, and transforming the data, we ensure high data quality and reliability. Ultimately, effective preprocessing prevents the "garbage in, garbage out" problem, enabling data mining techniques to extract meaningful, trustworthy insights.
